# Oct 28 model runs: side by side

All six runs use the same 274 reviewed clips, from two sessions: 97 from `20261027-224032-long-1` and 177 from
`20261028-155849-long-run2`. A clip counts as sweet when its distance is at most 4 cm. Always calling off-centre
scores **60.6%**.

## Verdict

**Keep the ridge distance regressor that is on the racket now.** Nothing tested beats it by more than noise. The
next gain will come from the labels and from new data, not from a different model.

## Grouped evaluation, all 274 clips

Five outer folds hold out whole 8-trigger blocks. I recomputed these from the saved out-of-fold predictions in
`20261028-scattering-multitask/combined_oof.npz`, and they match the run reports.

| Procedure | Accuracy | Balanced | Mean radial error | Accuracy, 3–5 cm band | Accuracy outside band |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Ridge, 156 features (deployed recipe)** | **67.2%** | 63.3% | 1.83 cm | 50.0% | 73.0% |
| Nested selection by distance error | 69.3% | 65.5% | 1.82 cm | 52.9% | 75.0% |
| Nested selection by balanced accuracy | 65.0% | 65.7% | 1.81 cm | 47.1% | 71.1% |
| Small multi-task network, current features | 65.0% | 65.7% | 1.80 cm | 48.6% | 70.6% |
| Scattering features, classical models | 67.2% | 63.3% | 1.98 cm | 51.4% | 72.5% |
| Multi-task network, scattering features | 59.9% | 60.4% | 2.02 cm | 42.9% | 65.7% |

- A group bootstrap of the ridge accuracy (2000 resamples of the 37 blocks) gives **59.6–74.8%**. The lower end sits
  at the always-off-centre baseline. The model's evidence is mostly in the distance (1.83 cm against 2.41 cm for
  the training median), not in the yes/no call.
- The best row, 69.3%, is 6 clips out of 274 better than ridge. The scattering run's paired bootstrap range for that
  gain crosses zero (−3.8 to +7.9 points).
- Earlier runs (`combined`, `combined-regularized`, `reviewed-combined`) trained the int8 MLP on 12 features. It
  scored 57.4% on the fixed test split, below the 61.1% baseline on that split, and was never promoted.

## Session to session (the case a new club session is)

Ridge at the deployed regularization, trained on one session and tested on the other:

| Train → test | Accuracy | Balanced | Mean error | Training-median error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Oct 27 (97) → Oct 28 (177) | 52.0% | 53.8% | 2.41 cm | 3.36 cm |
| Oct 28 (177) → Oct 27 (97) | 71.1% | 70.2% | 2.54 cm | 3.52 cm |

The audio does carry placement information across sessions: error drops by about 1 cm against the median. But the
predictions stay anchored near the training session's average. Mean true distance is 4.2 cm in the Oct 27 session
and 7.1 cm in the Oct 28 session, and the predictions only move partway toward the new average. The Oct 27 labels
used SAM scaling and the Oct 28 labels manual calibration (`scaled: false` in `metrics.json`). Confirm that both
put the centre and the cm scale in the same place before collecting more data. A systematic offset in either
session would look exactly like this.

## Checks run here

- `python3 ml/test_parity.py`: the firmware's C++ extractor and ridge (`firmware/src/sweetspot.cpp`) agree with
  Python on all 274 clips plus 22 synthetic ones. Max feature error 5e-7, max distance error 7e-7 cm, every decision
  identical. The run reports could not do this on Windows, which had no host compiler.
- No ESP32 build was made here, because PlatformIO isn't installed on this Mac.
- `ml/test_multitask_audio.py` fails to import. The vendored `kymatio` in `firmware/.pio/research-deps` imports
  `scipy.special.sph_harm`, which newer SciPy removed. That only affects the research scripts, not the firmware
  model.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Audit the label scale between the two sessions** (see above). This is the cheapest way to a better model.
2. **Show 3–5 cm as "close"** instead of forcing a yes/no call. Outside that band ridge is right 79% of the time, on
   56% of hits. Inside it, it's a coin flip. The racket computes `distance_cm` but `TBShot` carries only `sweet` and
   `off_pct`, which is 0 for every sweet call. So the 3–4 cm side of the band can't be seen in the app until the
   distance, or a third "close" state, goes on the wire.
3. **Refit ridge on all 274 clips** (`20261028-research/ridge_refit_all274.pkl`) and export it, after step 1. The
   current header was fitted on 175. Regenerate `distance_selftest.h` with it.
4. **Record a third session on another day**, ideally on court with the mic (see `docs/CLUB_2026-10-28.md`). Two
   sessions can't tell model error from session error.
