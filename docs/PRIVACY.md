# Tennis Buddy Trainer: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Tennis Buddy Trainer works with a sensor inside your tennis racket to measure your shots.

- **What it collects:** motion data from your racket (over Bluetooth), the shots and sessions computed from it,
  and, if you allow it on Apple Watch, heart rate and active calories during a tennis workout.
- **Where it's stored:** on your devices. If you're signed in to iCloud, sessions sync between your own devices
  through your private iCloud database. We (the developers) cannot see that data.
- **AI coach:** the Llama model runs entirely on your device. Your stats are never sent to a server.
  The app downloads the model's files from Hugging Face once; no personal data is sent in that request.
- **Health data** is used only to show your workout stats and is saved to Apple Health only with your permission.
  It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **Deleting data:** delete sessions in the app's History tab, or delete the app. Remove iCloud data in
  Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
